Privacy notice

Your words. Your data.

Updated 1 November 2026

Learning on this device

In device mode, your name, learning preferences, review history, bookmarks and writing are saved in this browser. Clearing browser data removes them. Export a backup from Preferences.

Your account and cloud progress

If you sign in, your email is handled by Supabase Authentication and your learning data is stored in the connected Supabase database. Row-level security restricts records to your account. You can export your data or permanently delete your account in Preferences.

Google sign-in supplies basic account identity information to Supabase Authentication so you can access your Verbum account. The connected Supabase database is hosted in Sydney, Australia. Cloudflare Pages serves this website.

Optional AI connections

AI is optional. Only when you request it, your selected exercise, sentence or word request is sent directly to OpenRouter and the model provider you choose, or to OpenAI if you connect an OpenAI API key. Their privacy and retention policies apply. Verbum keeps AI provider keys in tab session storage, not in its database.

Using Verbum

No advertising or analytics trackers are included. Avoid putting personal or sensitive information into practice sentences. Verbum is designed for secondary and university students.
